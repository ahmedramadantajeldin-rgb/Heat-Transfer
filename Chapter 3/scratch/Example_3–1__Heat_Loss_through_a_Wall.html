Example 3–1: Heat Loss through a Wall

Consider a 3-m-high, 5-m-wide, and 0.3-m-thick wall whose thermal conductivity is k = 0.9 W/m·K. On a certain day, the temperatures of the inner and the outer surfaces of the wall are measured to be 16°C and 2°C, respectively. Determine the rate of heat loss through the wall on that day.
Figure 3–11: Schematic for Example 3–1

Given

\(H = 3\;m\) \(W = 5\;m\) \(L = 0.3\;m\) \(k = 0.9\;W/m\cdot K\) \(T_1 = 16°C\) \(T_2 = 2°C\)

Solution

1
Wall area: \(A = (3)(5) = 15\;m^2\)
2
Using Eq. 3-3: \[ \dot{Q} = kA\frac{T_1 - T_2}{L} = (0.9)(15)\frac{(16 - 2)}{0.3} \]
3
Using thermal resistance: \[ R_{wall} = \frac{L}{kA} = \frac{0.3}{(0.9)(15)} = 0.02222\;°C/W \] \[ \dot{Q} = \frac{\Delta T}{R_{wall}} = \frac{14}{0.02222} \]
✅ Final Answer
\[ \dot{Q} = 630\;W \]

Convection & Radiation Resistance

§ 3-1 — Surface Thermal Resistances

Convection Resistance

باستخدام Newton's Law of Cooling: \(\dot{Q}_{conv} = hA_s(T_s - T_\infty)\)، يمكن إعادة كتابتها:

Convection Resistance — Eq. 3-7, 3-8
\[ \dot{Q}_{conv} = \frac{T_s - T_\infty}{R_{conv}} \qquad (W) \tag{3-7} \] \[ R_{conv} = \frac{1}{hA_s} \qquad (K/W) \tag{3-8} \]
Figure 3–4: Convection resistance at a surface
📝 Effect of Large \(h\)

إذا \(h \to \infty\) فإن \(R_{conv} \to 0\) وبالتالي \(T_s \approx T_\infty\). هذا يعني أن السطح لا يقدم تقريبًا أي مقاومة أمام Convection. هذه الحالة يتم الاقتراب منها عمليًا عند أسطح Boiling وCondensation.

المعادلة ليست مقتصرة على plane surfaces؛ يمكن استخدامها لأسطح بأي شكل بشرط أن يكون \(h = \text{constant and uniform}\).

Radiation Resistance

عندما يكون الجدار محاطًا بغاز، قد تكون تأثيرات Radiation مهمة. لسطح بـemissivity \(\varepsilon\) ومساحة \(A_s\):

Radiation Resistance — Eq. 3-9, 3-10
\[ \dot{Q}_{rad} = \varepsilon\sigma A_s(T_s^4 - T_{surr}^4) = h_{rad}A_s(T_s - T_{surr}) = \frac{T_s - T_{surr}}{R_{rad}} \tag{3-9} \] \[ R_{rad} = \frac{1}{h_{rad}A_s} \qquad (K/W) \tag{3-10} \]
Radiation Heat-Transfer Coefficient — Eq. 3-11
\[ h_{rad} = \varepsilon\sigma(T_s^2 + T_{surr}^2)(T_s + T_{surr}) \qquad (W/m^2\cdot K) \tag{3-11} \]
⚠️ Important

عند حساب \(h_{rad}\)، يجب أن تكون \(T_s\) و\(T_{surr}\) بوحدة Kelvin. كما أن \(h_{rad}\) يعتمد بشكل كبير على درجة الحرارة، بخلاف \(h_{conv}\).

Figure 3–5: Combined convection and radiation at a surface

Combined Heat-Transfer Coefficient

الـConvection وRadiation Resistances تكونان parallel. عندما \(T_{surr} \approx T_\infty\):

Combined Coefficient — Eq. 3-12
\[ h_{combined} = h_{conv} + h_{rad} \qquad (W/m^2\cdot K) \tag{3-12} \]

Thermal Resistance Network

§ 3-1 — Plane Wall with Convection on Both Sides

لـPlane Wall بسمك \(L\) ومساحة \(A\) ومعامل توصيل \(k\)، بين موائع عند \(T_{\infty1}\) و\(T_{\infty2}\) مع معاملات حمل \(h_1\) و\(h_2\)، حيث \(T_{\infty2} < T_{\infty1}\):

Figure 3–6: Thermal resistance network for a plane wall with convection on both sides

داخل الجدار تتغير درجة الحرارة linearly، بينما في الموائع تقترب درجة الحرارة asymptotically من \(T_{\infty1}\) و\(T_{\infty2}\). في steady conditions، نفس heat-transfer rate يمر خلال كل جزء:

Steady-State Energy Balance — Eq. 3-13
\[ \dot{Q} = h_1A(T_{\infty1} - T_1) = kA\frac{T_1 - T_2}{L} = h_2A(T_2 - T_{\infty2}) \tag{3-13} \]
Resistance Form — Eq. 3-14
\[ \dot{Q} = \frac{T_{\infty1} - T_1}{1/(h_1A)} = \frac{T_1 - T_2}{L/(kA)} = \frac{T_2 - T_{\infty2}}{1/(h_2A)} \tag{3-14} \]

Total Thermal Resistance

بجمع temperature differences ومقابلها resistances:

Total Heat Transfer — Eq. 3-15, 3-16
\[ \dot{Q} = \frac{T_{\infty1} - T_{\infty2}}{R_{total}} \qquad (W) \tag{3-15} \] \[ R_{total} = \frac{1}{h_1A} + \frac{L}{kA} + \frac{1}{h_2A} \qquad (K/W) \tag{3-16} \]
Figure 3–7: Mathematical identity for ratios
📝 Temperature Drop Across Any Layer

بما أن \(\dot{Q}\) ثابت في steady state:

\[ \Delta T = \dot{Q} \cdot R \qquad (^\circ C) \tag{3-17} \]

Temperature drop across a layer is proportional to the thermal resistance of that layer. كلما زادت \(R\)، زاد الانخفاض في درجة الحرارة عبرها.

Figure 3–8: Temperature drops proportional to thermal resistances

Overall Heat Transfer Coefficient \(U\)

Overall Heat Transfer Coefficient — Eq. 3-18, 3-19
\[ \dot{Q} = UA\Delta T \qquad (W) \tag{3-18} \] \[ UA = \frac{1}{R_{total}} \qquad (W/K) \tag{3-19} \]
📌 U-Factor

Overall heat-transfer coefficient = inverse of total thermal resistance per unit area.

وحدته: \(W/m^2 \cdot K\). يُستخدم بشكل خاص في Heat Exchangers وWindows (يُعرف باسم U-factor).

Determining Surface Temperatures

لا نحتاج لمعرفة surface temperatures مسبقًا لحساب \(\dot{Q}\). بعد حسابها:

Finding Surface Temperature — Eq. 3-20
\[ T_1 = T_{\infty1} - \dot{Q} \cdot R_{conv,1} \tag{3-20} \]
