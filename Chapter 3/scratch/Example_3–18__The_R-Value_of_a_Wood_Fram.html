Example 3–18: The R-Value of a Wood Frame Wall

The overall unit thermal resistance (R-value) and the overall heat transfer coefficient (U-factor) of a wood frame wall of a house are to be determined. The wall construction consists of 13-mm-thick wood siding, 13-mm-thick fiberboard sheathing, a 90-mm-thick cavity filled with fiberglass insulation between 38-mm × 90-mm wood studs spaced 400 mm on centers, and 13-mm-thick gypsum wallboard on the interior. The insulation path accounts for 75 percent of the wall area, and the stud path accounts for the remaining 25 percent. The house has a perimeter of 50 m and a height of 2.5 m, with glazing (windows and doors) accounting for 20 percent of the total gross wall area. Taking the indoor design temperature to be 20°C and the outdoor winter design temperature to be −4°C (wind speed 24 km/h), calculate the overall R-value, U-factor, and the rate of heat loss through the opaque walls.

Given

\(f_{ins} = 0.75\) (75% insulation area) \(f_{stud} = 0.25\) (25% stud area) \(\text{Perimeter} = 50\;\text{m}\) \(\text{Height} = 2.5\;\text{m}\) \(\text{Glazing Fraction} = 0.20\) \(T_i = 20\;{^\circ\text{C}}\) \(T_o = -4\;{^\circ\text{C}}\) \(\Delta T = 20 - (-4) = 24\;{^\circ\text{C}}\)

Solution

نلخص أولاً المقاومات الحرارية لوحدة المساحة لكل طبقة من طبقات الجدار عبر مساري انتقال الحرارة المتوازيين في الجدول التالي:

Construction Layer / Element Unit Thermal Resistance, \(R\;(\text{m}^2\cdot{^\circ\text{C}}/\text{W})\)
Between Studs (Insulation Path) At Studs (Framing Path)
1. Outside surface resistance (24 km/h wind) 0.030 0.030
2. Wood siding, 13 mm 0.14 0.14
3. Fiberboard sheathing, 13 mm 0.23 0.23
4. Fiberglass insulation, 90 mm 2.45 —
5. Wood stud, 38 mm × 90 mm — 0.63
6. Gypsum wallboard, 13 mm 0.079 0.079
7. Inside surface resistance (still air) 0.12 0.12
Total Unit Thermal Resistance (\(R\)) 3.05 1.23
← اسحب لرؤية المزيد →
1
U-factor for the insulation path (between studs): \[ U_{ins} = \frac{1}{R_{ins}} = \frac{1}{3.05\;\text{m}^2\cdot{^\circ\text{C}}/\text{W}} = 0.328\;\text{W}/(\text{m}^2\cdot\text{K}) \]
2
U-factor for the stud path (at framing): \[ U_{stud} = \frac{1}{R_{stud}} = \frac{1}{1.23\;\text{m}^2\cdot{^\circ\text{C}}/\text{W}} = 0.813\;\text{W}/(\text{m}^2\cdot\text{K}) \]
3
Overall area-weighted U-factor for the entire wall: \[ U_{overall} = f_{ins} U_{ins} + f_{stud} U_{stud} = (0.75)(0.328) + (0.25)(0.813) \] \[ U_{overall} = 0.246 + 0.203 = 0.449\;\text{W}/(\text{m}^2\cdot\text{K}) \]
4
Overall unit thermal resistance (R-value): \[ R_{overall} = \frac{1}{U_{overall}} = \frac{1}{0.449} \approx 2.23\;\text{m}^2\cdot{^\circ\text{C}}/\text{W} \]
5
Net opaque wall area calculation: إجمالي المساحة الجدارية للمبنى (Gross wall area): \[ A_{gross} = \text{Perimeter} \times \text{Height} = 50\;\text{m} \times 2.5\;\text{m} = 125\;\text{m}^2 \] وبما أن النوافذ والأبواب تشغل \(20\%\) من المساحة، فإن المساحة الصافية للجدار المصمت: \[ A_{wall} = (1 - 0.20) A_{gross} = (0.80)(125\;\text{m}^2) = 100\;\text{m}^2 \]
6
Rate of steady heat loss through the opaque wall: \[ \dot{Q}_{wall} = U_{overall} A_{wall} (T_i - T_o) \] \[ \dot{Q}_{wall} = (0.449\;\text{W}/\text{m}^2\cdot\text{K})(100\;\text{m}^2)(24\;{^\circ\text{C}}) = 1077.6\;\text{W} \approx 1078\;\text{W} = 1.078\;\text{kW} \]
✅ Final Answer
\[ R_{overall} \approx 2.23\;\text{m}^2\cdot{^\circ\text{C}}/\text{W}, \quad U_{overall} = 0.449\;\text{W}/(\text{m}^2\cdot\text{K}), \quad \dot{Q}_{wall} = 1.078\;\text{kW} \]

💬 Discussion

لاحظ التأثير الحاسم للجسور الحرارية (Thermal Bridging): على الرغم من أن العوارض الخشبية (Studs) تشغل فقط \(25\%\) من المساحة الكلية للجدار، إلا أنها مسؤولة عن انتقال: \[ \frac{0.203}{0.449} \times 100\% \approx 45.2\% \] من إجمالي الفقد الحراري للجدار! وهذا يبرز الأهمية القصوى لمراعاة الجسور الحرارية الإنشائية في كفاءة الطاقة للمباني وعدم الاكتفاء بحساب عازل الألياف الزجاجية بمفرده.
