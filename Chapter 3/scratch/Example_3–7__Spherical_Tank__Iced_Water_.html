Example 3–7: Spherical Tank (Iced Water)

A spherical tank (internal diameter 3 m, 2-cm-thick stainless steel, k = 15 W/m·K) contains iced water at 0°C in a room at 22°C. The outer surface is black. Given h1 = 80, h2 = 10 W/m²·K. Determine: (1) rate of heat transfer to the iced water; (2) amount of ice melted during 24 h.
Figure 3–28: Spherical tank with resistance network

Given

\(r_1 = 1.50\;m,\;r_2 = 1.52\;m\) \(A_1 = 28.3\;m^2,\;A_2 = 29.0\;m^2\) \(T_{\infty1} = 0°C,\;T_{\infty2} = 22°C\) \(h_{rad} = 5.34\;W/m^2\cdot K\) (assuming \(T_2 \approx 5°C\))

Solution

1
\[ R_i = \frac{1}{(80)(28.3)} = 0.000442\;°C/W \] \[ R_1 = \frac{1.52 - 1.50}{4\pi(15)(1.50)(1.52)} = 0.0000478\;°C/W \]
2
\[ R_o = \frac{1}{(10)(29.0)} = 0.00345\;°C/W \qquad R_{rad} = \frac{1}{(5.34)(29.0)} = 0.00646\;°C/W \]
3
\(R_o\) and \(R_{rad}\) are in parallel: \[ \frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \frac{1}{0.00345} + \frac{1}{0.00646} = 444.7 \quad \Rightarrow \quad R_{eq} = 0.00225\;°C/W \]
4
\[ R_{total} = 0.000442 + 0.0000478 + 0.00225 = 0.00274\;°C/W \] \[ \dot{Q} = \frac{22 - 0}{0.00274} \approx 8029\;W \]
5
Verify: \(T_2 = 22 - (8029)(0.00225) \approx 4°C\) ← close to assumed 5°C ✓
6
In 24 h: \(Q = (8.029)(24 \times 3600) = 693{,}700\;kJ\) \[ m = \frac{Q}{h_{if}} = \frac{693{,}700}{333.7} \approx 2079\;kg \]
✅ Final Answer
\[ \dot{Q} \approx 8029\;W \qquad m \approx 2079\;kg \approx 2\;\text{metric tons/day} \]
