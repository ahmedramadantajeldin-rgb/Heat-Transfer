<h4>Given</h4>
<div class="given-grid">
<span>\(r_1 = 1.50\;m,\;r_2 = 1.52\;m\)</span>
<span>\(A_1 = 28.3\;m^2,\;A_2 = 29.0\;m^2\)</span>
<span>\(T_{\infty1} = 0°C,\;T_{\infty2} = 22°C\)</span>
<span>\(h_{rad} = 5.34\;W/m^2\cdot K\) (assuming \(T_2 \approx 5°C\))</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="solution-steps">
<h4>Solution</h4>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">1</div><div class="step-content">
\[ R_i = \frac{1}{(80)(28.3)} = 0.000442\;°C/W \]
\[ R_1 = \frac{1.52 - 1.50}{4\pi(15)(1.50)(1.52)} = 0.0000478\;°C/W \]
</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">2</div><div class="step-content">
\[ R_o = \frac{1}{(10)(29.0)} = 0.00345\;°C/W \qquad R_{rad} = \frac{1}{(5.34)(29.0)} = 0.00646\;°C/W \]
</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">3</div><div class="step-content">
\(R_o\) and \(R_{rad}\) are in parallel:
\[ \frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \frac{1}{0.00345} + \frac{1}{0.00646} = 444.7 \quad \Rightarrow \quad R_{eq} = 0.00225\;°C/W \]
</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">4</div><div class="step-content">
\[ R_{total} = 0.000442 + 0.0000478 + 0.00225 = 0.00274\;°C/W \]
\[ \dot{Q} = \frac{22 - 0}{0.00274} \approx 8029\;W \]
</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">5</div><div class="step-content">
Verify: \(T_2 = 22 - (8029)(0.00225) \approx 4°C\) ← close to assumed 5°C ✓
</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">6</div><div class="step-content">
In 24 h: \(Q = (8.029)(24 \times 3600) = 693{,}700\;kJ\)
\[ m = \frac{Q}{h_{if}} = \frac{693{,}700}{333.7} \approx 2079\;kg \]
</div></div>
</div>
<div class="final-answer">
<div class="answer-label">✅ Final Answer</div>
\[ \dot{Q} \approx 8029\;W \qquad m \approx 2079\;kg \approx 2\;\text{metric tons/day} \]
</div>
</div></div>
