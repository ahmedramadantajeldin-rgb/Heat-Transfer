<div class="example-header"><h3>Example 3–13: Preventing Circuit Board Overheating</h3></div>
<div class="example-body">
<div class="problem-statement">
A 15 cm × 20 cm integrated circuit board is cooled by attaching 4-cm-long aluminum pin fins of square cross section (2 mm × 2 mm, <em>k</em> = 237 W/m·K). The surrounding air is at 25°C with a convection heat transfer coefficient of <em>h</em> = 20 W/m²·K. The board surface temperature must not exceed 85°C. Design a finned surface with an overall effectiveness of 3, and determine the appropriate number of fins needed.
</div>
