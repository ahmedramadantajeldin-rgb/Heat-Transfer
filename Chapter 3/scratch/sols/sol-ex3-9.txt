<div class="given-section">
<h4>Given</h4>
<div class="given-grid">
<span>\(r_1 = 1.5\;mm\)</span>
<span>\(r_2 = 3.5\;mm\)</span>
<span>\(\dot{Q} = VI = (8)(10) = 80\;W\)</span>
<span>\(k = 0.15\;W/m\cdot K\)</span>
<span>\(h = 12\;W/m^2\cdot K\)</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="solution-steps">
<h4>Solution</h4>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">1</div><div class="step-content">
\(A_2 = 2\pi(0.0035)(5) = 0.110\;m^2\)
\[ R_{conv} = \frac{1}{(12)(0.110)} = 0.758\;°C/W \qquad R_{plastic} = \frac{\ln(3.5/1.5)}{2\pi(5)(0.15)} = 0.180\;°C/W \]
</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">2</div><div class="step-content">
\[ R_{total} = 0.758 + 0.180 = 0.938\;°C/W \]
\[ T_1 = 30 + (80)(0.938) \approx 105°C \]
</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">3</div><div class="step-content">
Critical radius: \(r_{cr} = k/h = 0.15/12 = 12.5\;mm\). Since \(r_2 = 3.5\;mm < r_{cr}\), the wire is below critical radius.
</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">4</div><div class="step-content">
Doubling cover to 4 mm: \(T_1 \approx 90.6°C\). At \(r_2 = r_{cr} = 12.5\;mm\): \(T_{1,min} \approx 83°C\).
</div></div>
</div>
<div class="final-answer">
<div class="answer-label">✅ Final Answer</div>
\[ T_1 \approx 105°C \quad \text{(original)} \qquad T_1 \approx 90.6°C \quad \text{(doubled cover)} \]
</div>
