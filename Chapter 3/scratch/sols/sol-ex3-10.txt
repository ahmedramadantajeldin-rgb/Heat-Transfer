<div class="given-section">
<h4>Given</h4>
<div class="given-grid">
<span>\(T_c \le 85°C\)</span>
<span>\(T_\infty = 25°C\)</span>
<span>\(R_{case-ambient} = 20\;°C/W\)</span>
<span>\(\dot{W}_{rating} = 10\;W\)</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="solution-steps">
<h4>Solution</h4>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">1</div><div class="step-content">
From the thermal resistance model between the transistor case and ambient air:
\[ \dot{Q} = \frac{T_c - T_\infty}{R_{case-ambient}} = \frac{85 - 25}{20} = \frac{60}{20} = 3\;W \]
</div></div>
</div>
<div class="final-answer">
<div class="answer-label">✅ Final Answer</div>
\[ \dot{Q}_{max} = 3\;W \]
</div>
