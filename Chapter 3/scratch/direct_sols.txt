<span>\(\varepsilon_{fin,overall} = 3\) (required)</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="solution-steps">
<h4>Solution</h4>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">1</div><div class="step-content">
Fin parameter \(m\) and product \(mL\):
<br>For a pin fin of square cross section (\(a \times a\)):
\[ p = 4a = 4(0.002) = 0.008\;m, \qquad A_c = a^2 = (0.002)^2 = 4 \times 10^{-6}\;m^2 \]
\[ m = \sqrt{\frac{hp}{kA_c}} = \sqrt{\frac{4h}{ka}} = \sqrt{\frac{4(20)}{(237)(0.002)}} = \sqrt{\frac{80}{0.474}} = 12.99\;m^{-1} \]
\[ mL = (12.99\;m^{-1})(0.04\;m) = 0.5196 \]
</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">2</div><div class="step-content">
Individual fin efficiency (assuming adiabatic tip):
\[ \eta_{fin} = \frac{\tanh(mL)}{mL} = \frac{\tanh(0.5196)}{0.5196} = \frac{0.4774}{0.5196} = 0.919 \]
</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">3</div><div class="step-content">
Areas per fin:
<br>Surface area of one fin:
\[ A_{fin} = p L = 4(0.002)(0.04) = 3.2 \times 10^{-4}\;m^2 \]
Base area occupied by one fin:
\[ A_b = a^2 = (0.002)^2 = 4 \times 10^{-6}\;m^2 \]
</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">4</div><div class="step-content">
Heat transfer rates without fins and with \(n\) fins:
<br>Without fins:
\[ \dot{Q}_{no\;fin} = h A_{board} (T_b - T_\infty) = (20)(0.03)(85 - 25) = 36\;W \]
With \(n\) fins attached:
\[ A_{unfin} = A_{board} - n A_b = 0.03 - 4 \times 10^{-6} n \]
\[ \dot{Q}_{total} = h \left( A_{unfin} + \eta_{fin} A_{fin} \right) (T_b - T_\infty) \]
\[ \dot{Q}_{total} = (20)(60)\left[ (0.03 - 4 \times 10^{-6} n) + (0.919)(3.2 \times 10^{-4} n) \right] \]
\[ \dot{Q}_{total} = 1200\left[ 0.03 + 2.9008 \times 10^{-4} n \right] = 36 + 0.3481\,n \]
</div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="step-num">5</div><div class="step-content">
Determine the number of fins \(n\) from required \(\varepsilon_{fin,overall} = 3\):
\[ \varepsilon_{fin,overall} = \frac{\dot{Q}_{total}}{\dot{Q}_{no\;fin}} = \frac{36 + 0.3481\,n}{36} = 3 \]
\[ 36 + 0.3481\,n = 3 \times 36 = 108 \]
\[ 0.3481\,n = 108 - 36 = 72 \]
\[ n = \frac{72}{0.3481} \approx 206.8 \approx 207\;\text{fins} \]
</div></div>

=== sol-ex3-19 ===
id="sol-ex3-19"><div class="sol-inner">
<div class="given-section">
<h4>Given</h4>
<div class="given-grid">
<span>\(R_{overall,old} = 2.23\;\text{m}^2\cdot{^\circ\text{C}}/\text{W}\)</span>
<span>\(R_{fiberboard} = 0.23\;\text{m}^2\cdot{^\circ\text{C}}/\text{W}\)</span>
<span>\(R_{foam} = 0.98\;\text{m}^2\cdot{^\circ\text{C}}/\text{W}\)</span>
<span>\(A_{wall} = 100\;\text{m}^2\)</span>
<span>\(\Delta T = 24\;{^\circ\text{C}}\)</span>
</div>
</div>

<div class="solution-steps">
<h4>Solution</h4>

<div class="step"><div class="step-num">1</div><div class="step-content">
<strong>Analysis of the continuous layer modification:</strong><br>
نظرًا لأن لوح العزل الرغوي الصلب (<span class="term">Rigid foam insulation</span>) يغطي الجدار الخارجي باستمرار على كامل المساحة دون انقطاع، فإنه يُطبق بالتساوي فوق مسار العزل ومسار العوارض الخشبية على حد سواء (<span class="term">Continuous Exterior Insulation</span>).
</div></div>

<div class="step"><div class="step-num">2</div><div class="step-content">
<strong>Net increase in unit thermal resistance (\(\Delta R\)):</strong>
\[ \Delta R = R_{foam} - R_{fiberboard} = 0.98 - 0.23 = +0.75\;\text{m}^2\cdot{^\circ\text{C}}/\text{W} \]
</div></div>

<div class="step"><div class="step-num">3</div><div class="step-content">
<strong>New overall unit thermal resistance (\(R_{new}\)):</strong>
\[ R_{new} = R_{overall,old} - R_{fiberboard} + R_{foam} = 2.23 - 0.23 + 0.98 = 2.98\;\text{m}^2\cdot{^\circ\text{C}}/\text{W} \]
</div></div>

<div class="step"><div class="step-num">4</div><div class="step-content">
<strong>Percentage increase in wall R-value:</strong>
\[ \text{Percentage increase} = \frac{R_{new} - R_{overall,old}}{R_{overall,old}} \times 100\% \]
\[ \text{Percentage increase} = \frac{2.98 - 2.23}{2.23} \times 100\% = \frac{0.75}{2.23} \times 100\% \approx 33.6\% \approx 34\% \]
</div></div>

<div class="step"><div class="step-num">5</div><div class="step-content">
<strong>New overall U-factor and heat loss rate:</strong>
\[ U_{new} = \frac{1}{R_{new}} = \frac{1}{2.98\;\text{m}^2\cdot{^\circ\text{C}}/\text{W}} \approx 0.336\;\text{W}/(\text{m}^2\cdot\text{K}) \]
\[ \dot{Q}_{new} = U_{new} A_{wall} \Delta T = (0.336\;\text{W}/\text{m}^2\cdot\text{K})(100\;\text{m}^2)(24\;{^\circ\text{C}}) = 806.4\;\text{W} \approx 0.806\;\text{kW} \]
مقدار التوفير في الفقد الحراري:
\[ \Delta \dot{Q} = 1078 - 806.4 = 271.6\;\text{W} \quad (25.2\%\;\text{reduction}) \]
</div></div>
