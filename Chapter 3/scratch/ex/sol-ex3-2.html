Example 3–2: Heat Loss through a Single-Pane Window

Consider a 0.8-m-high and 1.5-m-wide glass window with a thickness of 8 mm and a thermal conductivity of k = 0.78 W/m·K. Determine the steady rate of heat transfer through this glass window and the temperature of its inner surface for a day during which the room is maintained at 20°C while the temperature of the outdoors is −10°C. Take the heat transfer coefficients on the inner and outer surfaces of the window to be h1 = 10 W/m²·K and h2 = 40 W/m²·K, which includes the effects of radiation.
Figure 3–12: Schematic for Example 3–2

Given

\(A = 0.8 \times 1.5 = 1.2\;m^2\) \(L = 0.008\;m\) \(k = 0.78\;W/m\cdot K\) \(T_{\infty1} = 20°C\) \(T_{\infty2} = -10°C\) \(h_1 = 10\;W/m^2\cdot K\) \(h_2 = 40\;W/m^2\cdot K\)

Solution

1
Individual Resistances: \[ R_i = \frac{1}{h_1 A} = \frac{1}{(10)(1.2)} = 0.08333\;°C/W \] \[ R_{glass} = \frac{L}{kA} = \frac{0.008}{(0.78)(1.2)} = 0.00855\;°C/W \] \[ R_o = \frac{1}{h_2 A} = \frac{1}{(40)(1.2)} = 0.02083\;°C/W \]
