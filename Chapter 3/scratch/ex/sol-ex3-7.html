Example 3–7: Spherical Tank (Iced Water)

A spherical tank (internal diameter 3 m, 2-cm-thick stainless steel, k = 15 W/m·K) contains iced water at 0°C in a room at 22°C. The outer surface is black. Given h1 = 80, h2 = 10 W/m²·K. Determine: (1) rate of heat transfer to the iced water; (2) amount of ice melted during 24 h.
