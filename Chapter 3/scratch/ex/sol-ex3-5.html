Example 3–5: Contact Resistance of Transistors

Four identical power transistors with aluminum casing are attached on one side of a 1-cm-thick 20 cm × 20 cm copper plate (k = 386 W/m·K) by screws that exert an average pressure of 6 MPa. Each transistor base area is 8 cm², placed at the center of a 10 cm × 10 cm quarter section. Interface roughness ≈ 1.5 μm. All heat must dissipate to the ambient at 20°C through the back surface (h = 25 W/m²·K). If case temperature must not exceed 70°C, determine the maximum power each transistor can dissipate and the temperature jump at the case-plate interface.
Figure 3–18: Schematic for Example 3–5

Given

\(L_{plate} = 0.01\;m\) \(k_{Cu} = 386\;W/m\cdot K\) \(A_c = 8 \times 10^{-4}\;m^2\) \(A = 0.01\;m^2\) \(h_c = 42{,}000\;W/m^2\cdot K\) \(h_o = 25\;W/m^2\cdot K\) \(T_{case} = 70°C,\;T_\infty = 20°C\)

Solution

1
\[ R_{interface} = \frac{1}{h_c A_c} = \frac{1}{(42{,}000)(8 \times 10^{-4})} = 0.0298\;°C/W \]
