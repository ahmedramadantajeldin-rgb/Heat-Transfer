Example 3–4: Equivalent Thickness for Contact Resistance

The thermal contact conductance at the interface of two 1-cm-thick aluminum plates is measured to be 11,000 W/m²·K. Determine the thickness of the aluminum plate whose thermal resistance is equal to the thermal resistance of the interface between the plates.

Given

\(h_c = 11{,}000\;W/m^2\cdot K\) \(k_{Al} = 237\;W/m\cdot K\)

Solution

1
\[ R_c = \frac{1}{h_c} = \frac{1}{11{,}000} = 0.909 \times 10^{-4}\;m^2\cdot K/W \]
