Example 3–9: Electric Wire with Plastic Insulation

An electric wire (diameter 3 mm, length 5 m) is covered with a 2-mm-thick plastic layer (k = 0.15 W/m·K). Current I = 10 A, voltage drop V = 8 V. Ambient at 30°C, h = 12 W/m²·K. Determine: (1) the interface temperature; (2) the effect of doubling the cover thickness.
Figure 3–32: Schematic for Example 3–9

Given

\(r_1 = 1.5\;mm\) \(r_2 = 3.5\;mm\) \(\dot{Q} = VI = (8)(10) = 80\;W\) \(k = 0.15\;W/m\cdot K\) \(h = 12\;W/m^2\cdot K\)

Solution

1
\(A_2 = 2\pi(0.0035)(5) = 0.110\;m^2\) \[ R_{conv} = \frac{1}{(12)(0.110)} = 0.758\;°C/W \qquad R_{plastic} = \frac{\ln(3.5/1.5)}{2\pi(5)(0.15)} = 0.180\;°C/W \]
2
