Example 3–3: Heat Loss through Double-Pane Windows

Consider a 0.8-m-high and 1.5-m-wide double-pane window consisting of two 4-mm-thick layers of glass (k = 0.78 W/m·K) separated by a 10-mm-wide stagnant air space (k = 0.026 W/m·K). Determine the steady rate of heat transfer through this double-pane window and the temperature of its inner surface for the same conditions as Example 3-2.
Figure 3–13: Schematic for Example 3–3

Given

\(A = 1.2\;m^2\) \(L_1 = L_3 = 0.004\;m\) \(L_2 = 0.01\;m\) \(k_{glass} = 0.78\;W/m\cdot K\) \(k_{air} = 0.026\;W/m\cdot K\) \(h_1 = 10,\;h_2 = 40\;W/m^2\cdot K\)

Solution

1
Individual Resistances: \[ R_i = 0.08333\;°C/W \quad R_o = 0.02083\;°C/W \] \[ R_{glass} = \frac{0.004}{(0.78)(1.2)} = 0.00427\;°C/W \quad (\text{each layer}) \] \[ R_{air} = \frac{0.01}{(0.026)(1.2)} = 0.3205\;°C/W \]
